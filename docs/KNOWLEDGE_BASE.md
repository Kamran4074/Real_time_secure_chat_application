## 1. About ChatWave

ChatWave is a web app for private, real-time conversations between two people. Messages arrive
instantly, and you can see when the other person is online, typing, has received your message and
has read it.

- **Who it is for:** anyone who wants a simple, private one-on-one chat in the browser.
- **Platforms:** any modern web browser on desktop or mobile (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari). There
  is no app to install.
- **Price:** free.
- **Languages:** English.

## 2. Features: what ChatWave can and cannot do

### Available today

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| Sign up with email | Full name, username, email, gender and a password |
| Sign in with Google or GitHub | One click; no password needed |
| One-on-one messaging | Text messages delivered instantly |
| Recent chats | Your conversations, most recent first, with an unread count |

ChatWave does **not** currently offer:

- editing or deleting a sent message
- deleting a conversation
- deleting your account by yourself (see [6.7](#67-your-rights-and-choices) for how to request it)
- changing your email address
- turning off read receipts, "last seen" or online status
- end-to-end encryption (see [7.2](#72-how-your-data-is-protected))
- mobile apps, desktop apps or push notifications when the site is closed
- reporting a user from inside the app (use support instead, see [13](#13-support-and-escalation))
- two-factor authentication

## 3. Accounts and signing in

### 3.1 Creating an account with email

You need:

| Field | Rules |
|---|---|
| Full name | Up to 60 characters |
| Username | 3–20 characters: letters, numbers, `_` or `.`; must be unique (capital letters don't make a new one: `Aisha` and `aisha` are the same) |
| Email | A valid address, not already used by another account |
| Gender | Male, female or other |
| Password | 6–72 characters; spaces are allowed |

You are signed in straight after registering. A profile picture is generated for you automatically,
and you can replace it later.

### 3.2 Signing in with Google or GitHub

- Click the **Google** or **GitHub** button on the login or sign-up page.
- Your Google / GitHub account must have a **verified email address**.
- If a ChatWave account with the same email already exists, the Google / GitHub sign-in is **linked**
  to it: you reach the same account and chats.
- A new account gets a username made from your name or email (numbers are added if it's taken).
  You can change it in your profile.
- These accounts have **no ChatWave password** at first. You can set one in
  **Profile → Account → Set a password** to also sign in with email and password.
- If a button shows "Soon" and is greyed out, that sign-in option is not switched on yet.

### 3.3 Staying signed in

- A sign-in lasts **7 days** on that browser, then you are asked to log in again.
- You are also signed out when:
  - you click **Log out** (only that device),
  - you use **Log out from all devices**,
  - you or someone else changes the password (other devices are signed out),
  - the password is reset with a reset link (every device, including the current one).

### 3.4 Forgot password

1. On the login page click **Forgot password?** and enter your email.
2. If an account exists, an email titled **"Reset your ChatWave password"** is sent.
   For privacy, the page shows the same message whether or not the email has an account.
3. The link is valid for **30 minutes** and works **once**. Only the newest link works.
4. Choose a new password (6–72 characters). You are then signed out everywhere and log in with the
   new password.

The email may take a few minutes. Check the spam / junk folder.
Accounts created with Google / GitHub that never set a password should simply use
Google / GitHub to sign in, or set a password from the profile once signed in.

### 3.5 Changing your password

**Profile → Account → Change password.** Enter your current password and the new one twice. Other
devices are signed out; the device you used stays signed in.

### 3.6 Logging out

- **Log out** (icon at the bottom of the chat list): this device only.
- **Log out from all devices** (**Profile → Account**): every device, including this one.

## 4. Using ChatWave

### 4.1 Starting a chat

1. Type at least **2 characters** of a person's name or username in the search box
   (`@kamran` and `kamran` search the same).
2. Results match the **start** of a username or the start of any word in a name
   ("joh" finds "Alice Johnson"). Up to 20 people are shown.
3. Click the person and send a message. The chat then appears in **Recent chats**.

You won't find yourself, or people who have blocked you.

### 4.2 Messages

- Text only, up to **2,000 characters** per message. A counter appears near the limit.
- Line breaks, emoji and any language are fine.
- Messages appear instantly for the other person if they are online, otherwise the next time they
  open ChatWave.
- Messages can't be edited or deleted after sending.
- Older messages load when you scroll up (or click **Load older messages**), 30 at a time.

### 4.3 Read receipts (ticks)

| Ticks | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ✓ (one grey tick) | Sent to the server; the other person hasn't been online since |
| ✓✓ (two grey ticks) | Delivered to at least one of their open devices |
| ✓✓ (two blue ticks) | Seen: they opened the chat while the message was there |

Read receipts are always on for everyone and can't be turned off.

### 4.4 Online, last seen and typing

- **Online** (green) means the person has ChatWave open in at least one tab.
- **Last seen …** shows when their last tab closed, for example "last seen just now",
  "last seen today at 9:05 am", "last seen yesterday at 9:00 pm" or "last seen 2 Oct at 8:00 am".
- A short refresh doesn't show you as offline: going offline is announced after a few seconds.
- **typing…** appears while they type and disappears about 2 seconds after they stop.
- Online status, last seen and typing are **hidden between two people when either one has blocked
  the other**.

### 4.5 Your profile

Open it by clicking your name at the bottom of the chat list.

| Item | Rules |
|---|---|
| Name | Up to 60 characters, can't be empty |
| Username | 3–20 characters (letters, numbers, `_`, `.`), must be unique |
| About (bio) | Up to 140 characters, optional |
| Photo | JPG, PNG or WebP, under **5 MB**; stored as a 400×400 square, cropped around the face |
| Email | Shown only to you; can't be changed |

- **Remove** puts back the generated picture.
- If **Upload photo** is missing, photo upload is temporarily unavailable.
- Other people see your name, username, photo, bio and last seen. They **never** see your email.

### 4.6 Blocking

Block from the chat: click the person's name at the top → **Block**.

When you block someone:

- they can't send you messages,
- you can't send them messages until you unblock them (a notice replaces the message box),
- neither of you sees the other's online status, last seen or typing,
- they can't find you in search,
- they are **not told** that you blocked them (they see a general "You can't message this user").

Your existing message history stays visible to both of you.

**Unblock** from the same place, from the notice in the chat, or from
**Profile → Blocked contacts**.

## 5. Limits and fair use

Limits protect everyone from spam and abuse. If you hit one, wait a little and try again.

| Action | Limit | Message you see |
|---|---|---|
| Sign-in, sign-up, password reset, change password, Google/GitHub sign-in completion | 20 attempts per 15 minutes per network | "Too many attempts, please try again later" |
| Sending messages | 30 per minute | "You're sending messages too fast, please wait a moment" |
| Searching people | 60 per minute | "Too many searches, please wait a moment" |
| All other requests | 600 per 15 minutes per network | "Too many requests, please slow down" |
| Message length | 2,000 characters | "Message must be under 2000 characters" |
| Profile photo | 5 MB, JPG / PNG / WebP | "Photo must be under 5 MB" / "Use a JPG, PNG or WebP image" |

People sharing one network (an office or a college Wi-Fi) share the per-network limits.

## 7. Security policy

### 7.1 Protecting your account

- Choose a password you don't use anywhere else.
- Use **Log out from all devices** if you signed in on a shared or lost device.
- ChatWave staff will **never** ask for your password, reset link or sign-in code.
- Reset links come only from ChatWave's emails, are valid for 30 minutes and work once.

### 7.2 How your data is protected

- All traffic between your browser and ChatWave is encrypted with **HTTPS / TLS**.
- Passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes.
- Sign-in uses signed tokens that expire after 7 days and can be revoked (logout, password change).
- Reset links and Google / GitHub sign-in codes are stored only as hashes, expire quickly and
  work once.
- Rate limits slow down password guessing and spam (see [5](#5-limits-and-fair-use)).
- Every request input is validated; messages are always shown as plain text, so code in a message
  can't run.
- Profile photos can only be your own upload to our storage.
- The website sends strict browser security headers (Content Security Policy, no framing).
- Messages are **encrypted in transit but not end-to-end encrypted**: they are stored on our
  database so they can be delivered later and shown as history.

### 7.3 Reporting a security problem

Found a vulnerability? Email `[SECURITY EMAIL]` with the steps to reproduce it. Please don't access
other people's data, disrupt the service or share the issue publicly before it is fixed. We will
acknowledge your report within `[NUMBER]` business days.

## 10. Frequently asked questions

**Is ChatWave free?**
Yes.

**Do I need to install anything?**
No. It runs in your web browser on a computer or phone.

**Can I create a group chat?**
Not yet. ChatWave is for one-on-one chats only.

**Can I send photos, videos or files?**
No, messages are text only. You can only upload a profile photo.

**Can I make voice or video calls?**
No.

**Can I edit or delete a message I sent?**
No. Messages can't be changed after sending.

**Can I delete a conversation?**
Not at the moment.

**How do I delete my account?**
There is no delete button yet. Email `[PRIVACY / GRIEVANCE OFFICER EMAIL]` from the email address of
your account and ask for deletion.

**Can I change my email address?**
No, the email can't be changed at the moment.

**Can I change my username?**
Yes: **Profile → Username → Save changes**. It must be 3–20 characters and not taken.

**Who can see my email?**
Only you.

**Can people find me?**
Yes, anyone with an account can find you by searching your name or username, unless you have
blocked them.

**Can I hide my online status, last seen or read receipts?**
No, these are always on. Blocking someone hides them from that person.

**Will someone know if I block them?**
They are not notified. If they try to message you they see "You can't message this user".

**What do the ticks mean?**
One grey tick: sent. Two grey ticks: delivered. Two blue ticks: seen. See [4.3](#43-read-receipts-ticks).

**Why is there only one tick on my message?**
The other person hasn't opened ChatWave since you sent it. It turns into two ticks as soon as they do.

**Are my messages encrypted?**
They are encrypted while travelling over the internet (HTTPS) but stored on our servers so they can
be delivered and shown as history. ChatWave is not end-to-end encrypted.

**I signed up with Google. Can I also log in with email and password?**
Yes. Go to **Profile → Account → Set a password**, then log in with your email and that password.

**I have an email account and now signed in with Google using the same email. Is that a new account?**
No. It is linked to your existing account, so you keep your chats.

**Does ChatWave have a mobile app?**
Not yet. Use the website in your phone's browser.

**Do I get notifications when ChatWave is closed?**
No. A sound plays when a message arrives in the chat you have open.

**How long do I stay logged in?**
Up to 7 days on the same browser, unless you log out or change your password.

**How many characters can a message have?**
2,000.

**What photo can I upload?**
JPG, PNG or WebP under 5 MB. It's cropped to a square around your face.

## 11. Troubleshooting

### 11.1 The site is slow or says "Server is starting…"

ChatWave's server goes to sleep when nobody has used it for a while. The first visit wakes it up,
which takes **30–60 seconds**. A yellow banner with a countdown shows this. Wait for
"Server is ready ✓". If it says **"Server is not responding."** after about 2 minutes, click **Retry**
or reload the page.

### 11.2 I can't log in

- Check the email and password. The message "Invalid email or password" appears for both a wrong
  email and a wrong password, for security.
- Did you sign up with Google or GitHub? Use that button instead, or set a password from your
  profile after signing in that way.
- Forgot your password? Use **Forgot password?** on the login page.
- "Too many attempts, please try again later": wait 15 minutes.

### 11.3 I didn't get the password-reset email

- Wait a few minutes and check your spam / junk / promotions folders.
- Make sure you typed the email of your ChatWave account.
- Request a new link; only the newest link works.
- Still nothing? Contact `[SUPPORT EMAIL]`.

### 11.4 "This reset link is invalid or has expired"

The link is older than 30 minutes, was already used, or a newer one was requested. Click
**Request a new link** and use the newest email.

### 11.5 Google / GitHub sign-in fails

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Sign-in was cancelled." | You pressed Cancel on the Google / GitHub page. Try again. |
| "Sign-in expired. Please try again." | Start again from the ChatWave login page, in the same browser, without switching tabs or devices. Allow cookies for the site. |
| "Your account needs a verified email address." | Verify your email in your Google / GitHub account settings, then try again. |
| "This sign-in option is not available right now." | Use email and password, or the other provider. |
| "Sign-in link expired, please try again" | The final step took longer than a minute. Try again. |
| "Could not sign you in. Please try again." | A temporary problem. Try again later or contact support. |

### 11.6 My messages don't arrive / ticks don't update

- Reload the page. The live connection reconnects by itself, but a reload forces it.
- Check your internet connection.
- If you see "You can't message this user", that person has blocked you or you have blocked them.
- If you see "You're sending messages too fast", wait a minute.

### 11.7 I can't find someone in search

- Type at least 2 characters, starting from the beginning of their username or of a word in their
  name.
- You can't find people who have blocked you.
- Check the spelling; up to 20 results are shown, so make the search more specific.

### 11.8 I was suddenly logged out

This happens when: 7 days passed, you used **Log out from all devices**, your password was changed
or reset, or you cleared your browser data. Log in again. If you didn't change your password
yourself, reset it right away and contact support.

### 11.9 My photo won't upload

- Use a JPG, PNG or WebP under 5 MB.
- If there is no **Upload photo** button, uploads are temporarily unavailable; try later.

### 11.10 "Username already taken"

Someone else has that username (capital letters don't count). Choose another one.

## 14. Guidelines for the support assistant

These rules are for an AI assistant answering ChatWave users.

**Scope**
- Answer only from this knowledge base. If something isn't covered, say you don't know and point to
  support ([13](#13-support-and-escalation)). Never invent features, limits, dates or policies.
- If a user asks for a feature listed in [2 · Not available](#not-available-do-not-promise-these),
  say clearly that it isn't available today. Don't promise that it will come or when.

**Safety and privacy**
- Never ask for, accept or repeat passwords, reset links or sign-in codes. If a user pastes one,
  tell them to change their password.
- The assistant can't see accounts or messages and can't change anything. It can't unblock,
  restore, delete or look up users. Send such requests to support.
- Never confirm whether a particular email or username has an account.
- Harassment or threats: tell the user to block the person ([4.6](#46-blocking)) and report them to
  support. Someone in immediate danger should contact local emergency services.
- Account deletion, data copies or corrections go to the privacy contact.

**Style**
- Short, friendly, step by step. Use the exact button names from the app (in bold).
- Ask one clarifying question when the problem is unclear (for example "Did you sign up with email or
  with Google?").
- Reply in the user's language if possible. The app itself is in English, so keep button names in
  English.

**Escalate to a human when**
- the user suspects their account was taken over,
- they report abuse, illegal content or a security vulnerability,
- they ask for account deletion, a data copy, or a legal / privacy question,
- the same error keeps happening after the troubleshooting steps (ask for the request ID).
